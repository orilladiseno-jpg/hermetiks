Privacy Policy

HERMETIKS Soundboard, by Orilla Estudio Creativo, part of HERMETIKS COLLECTIVE, Chile. Last updated: 26 September 2026.

The short version

HERMETIKS Soundboard runs entirely on your computer. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data, and the app has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or advertising.

The application

  • Keyboard hook. To react to your shortcuts while a game has focus, the app installs a Windows low-level keyboard hook. Each key press is only compared with your configured shortcuts and discarded. Keystrokes are never stored, logged or transmitted.
  • Network access. The app connects to the internet only when you import audio from a link (a direct audio URL, or a page supported by yt-dlp such as YouTube). Nothing else is sent anywhere.
  • Other applications' volume. If you enable Duck, the app temporarily lowers the volume of the applications you list (default: Spotify) through the Windows audio session API and restores it afterwards.
  • Now Playing overlay. The overlay built into the app reads what is playing from the Windows media controls and serves it only to your own computer (127.0.0.1); nothing leaves your machine.
  • Local files. Settings and imported sounds are stored on your computer in %APPDATA%\Hermetiks. The uninstaller offers to delete them.

This website

  • The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party trackers.
  • Your language choice is remembered in your browser's local storage only.
  • The download button and version label read public release information from GitHub's API (api.github.com) in your browser. GitHub may log that request under its own privacy statement. Downloads are served by GitHub.
  • The live demo plays short audio clips in your browser; no audio or key presses leave your device.
  • The optional web widget at /spotify/ connects your browser directly to Spotify (accounts.spotify.com and api.spotify.com) with a read-only "currently playing" permission that you grant to your own Spotify app. Its settings and tokens are stored in your browser only; we never receive them.
