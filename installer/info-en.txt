This is free and open source software released under the MIT License (shown on the next page).

What it does
  - Plays your sounds when you press keys, even while a game is in focus.
  - Sends the audio to the output device you choose, so it mixes with Spotify or Chrome.

Permissions and privacy
  - It installs for the current user only and does not need administrator rights.
  - It uses a Windows keyboard hook to detect your shortcuts. Keystrokes are only compared with your shortcuts; they are never stored or sent anywhere.
  - It collects no data, has no telemetry and shows no ads.
  - The only network access happens when you import audio from a link.
  - It can lower the volume of other apps (for example Spotify) while a clip plays. This can be turned off.
